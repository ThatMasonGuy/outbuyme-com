# Product foundation

## Proposition

Outbuy Me is one public webpage with one current owner. A player pays the current one-off takeover price to control the content inside the permanent Outbuy Me frame. All payments go to the platform owner. Players receive no remuneration and the product is not represented as an investment.

## Visual thesis

A page torn from a shared school notebook: warm ruled paper, highlighter colour, pen annotations and compact, legible editorial type. The product chrome stays calm while the owner-controlled canvas can change personality without hiding the game.

The platform chrome is deliberately stable. Owner content is expressive but cannot replace, cover or imitate the takeover controls.

## Draft-first choice

The studio is available before purchase. Both formats produce a versioned `OwnerContent` document and use the same preview, moderation and publishing pipeline. Authentication and payment happen when the user is ready to publish.

### Quick promo

For a buyer who wants to publish immediately.

- avatar or logo
- name or handle
- headline
- 400-character pitch
- an ordered list of up to 100 links
- one of four safe paper themes

### Build the page

For a buyer who wants the canvas.

The v1 builder stores a page as movable blocks. Owners can add display text, body text, handwriting, images, buttons, highlighter marks and shapes from the toolbox. Items move from the point where they are grabbed and resize from a visible canvas handle. Arrow keys move the selected item; Shift plus arrow keys resize it. Owners can also change styles, colours and overlapping order.

Owners may switch between formats and update their published revision while they remain the current owner. When outbought, the published revision becomes an immutable archive snapshot and a separate editable copy returns to draft mode for a future takeover.

Former owners can request removal of their archived page. Removal hides the page and public owner details without rewriting the takeover count or transaction record.

## Voice

Direct, competitive and slightly unruly. Routine controls stay specific and calm. Errors, payments, reports and moderation do not use jokes.

Preferred terms:

- `Take over · $5 USD`
- `Quick promo`
- `Build the page`
- `Current owner`
- `Page history`
- `Report the page`

Avoid implying ownership of the domain or a financial return. Customer-facing terms should ultimately describe temporary content control or a temporary publication licence.

## Accessibility requirements

- WCAG 2.2 AA baseline
- permanent takeover control remains keyboard and touch operable
- editor inputs have persistent labels and text errors
- drag interactions must have a non-drag alternative when introduced
- desktop and mobile previews are both required before publishing
- owner-selected colours cannot reduce platform-frame contrast
- owner animation must support pause and reduced-motion behaviour
